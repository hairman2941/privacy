Privacy Policy

Super Little Things Uploader
Effective date: October 17, 2026

Super Little Things Uploader (the "App") is a private tool used by the operator of the YouTube channels "Super Little Things" and "ココグチ (Cocoguchi)" to upload videos that the operator created to the operator's own YouTube channels. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and to confirm which YouTube channel is connected. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information the App accesses

With your permission through Google's sign-in screen, the App accesses only:

  • Upload permission (youtube.upload): to upload video files, titles, descriptions, tags and language settings that you choose.
  • Read-only channel information (youtube.readonly): the channel ID and channel name, used only to confirm that a video is uploaded to the correct channel.

The App does not access your Google account password, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any other data.

3. How information is stored

  • The App runs only on the operator's own computer. There is no App server or website that receives your data.
  • Sign-in tokens issued by Google and the connected channel ID and name are saved only on that computer.
  • The App does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising tools.

4. Sharing of information

The App does not sell, share, or disclose any information to third parties. Information is sent only to Google/YouTube, as needed to upload videos.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Stored tokens and channel information are kept only while the App is in use.
  • You can revoke the App's access at any time from the Google security settings page.
  • When access is revoked, or on request by email, the stored tokens and channel information are deleted from the operator's computer within 7 days.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
