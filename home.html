What this app does

Super Little Things Uploader is a private command-line tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Super Little Things and ココグチ (Cocoguchi). It uploads videos that the owner created to the owner's own channels.

  • Signs in with Google OAuth and lets the owner choose which of their channels to use.
  • Confirms the connected channel before each upload, to prevent uploading to the wrong channel.
  • Uploads a video with its title, description, tags, and language as private.
  • Schedules public release of the owner's videos (status.publishAt).

Who can use it

This tool is not publicly available. It runs only on the owner's own computer and is not offered to other users. It does not access, display, or analyze anyone else's content or data.
